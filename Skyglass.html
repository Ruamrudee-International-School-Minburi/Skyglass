How to play

Your house never stops moving. Hunters chase from behind. Outrun them, outgun them, or both.

Walking around your house

W A S D
Walk. Hold Shift to run
Space
Jump, or rise in a lift hatch between floors (C to go down)
Left mouse
Harvester beam: hold on floating scrap, crates, ice and shards to pull them in
Right mouse
Kinetic pulse from the harvester, hits hunters (uses energy)
E
Use things: take the helm, harvest the planter, collect water, rest in bed
F / G
Eat a food ration / drink a water ration
R
Repair the hull with metal
B
Build: rooms, engines, shields, turrets and more
V
Outside view of your house

At the helm

W / S
Throttle up or down
A / D
Steer left or right
Space / C
Climb or descend
Shift
Afterburner (uses energy)
T
Hand control to the autopilot, which only flies straight ahead
E
Leave the seat. The autopilot takes over

Building

Left mouse
Place. Aim at a wall, roof or floor
R
Rotate furniture and devices
Wheel
Switch item
Q
Deconstruct tool (refunds 60%)
X
Put the build tool away

Speed comes from thrust against weight. Every room adds weight, so pair new rooms with engines. If weight passes lift, the house slows hard.
